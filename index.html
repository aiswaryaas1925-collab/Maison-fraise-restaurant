Our little story

A tiny bakery with a big strawberry obsession.

Maison Fraise began as a weekend baking project: one tiny oven, a handwritten recipe book, and a conviction that a pastry can change the shape of an ordinary day.

Today, we are your neighbourhood place for slow mornings, cake-filled milestones, and a little pink box to take home.
